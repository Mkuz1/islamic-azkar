Questions

Where do the azkar come from?
From the Fortress of the Muslim, the Quran and hadith graded sahih or hasan. Each dhikr shows its reference.
Is it really free?
Yes. There are no ads, no purchases and no subscription. The app is a sadaqa jariyah.
Do I need an account?
No. Everything stays on your device. Sync is optional and uses a private code, never your name or email.
Can I use it in English or French?
Yes. The app speaks Arabic, English and French, with transliteration and meaning under each dhikr.
How are prayer times calculated?
On your device, for your location or a chosen city, with nine methods including Umm al Qura and Dubai, and Hanafi Asr.
How do I delete my data?
Only synced records are kept on our server, and you can erase them at islamicazkar.net/delete at any time.
